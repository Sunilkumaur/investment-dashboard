Tax settings

Splitting the exit across 2 or more financial years (e.g. sell part in March, the rest in April) gives you the ₹1.25L equity exemption more than once. Within each category, losses are netted against gains.

Charges

Mutual funds bought through Direct plans have no brokerage and no DP charge. GOLDBEES pays no STT on non-equity ETFs.

Existing holdings (optional)

Existing holdings grow at the gold rate and the blended equity rate. They're taxed as long-term when the horizon is over 1 year.

Your planning cap is 10–15%.
How the numbers are calculated
  • Each monthly SIP instalment is a separate lot. A lot's value = amount × (1 + CAGR)(months held / 12).
  • Lots held for 12 months or less at exit are short-term; older lots are long-term (FIFO, the same as your GOLDBEES tracking).
  • Exit load is charged on lots still inside the fund's exit-load window. It reduces both the proceeds and the taxable gain.
  • Equity LTCG tax = rate × max(0, LTCG − exemption × N financial years). Gold ETF LTCG has no exemption. Cess is added on top. Surcharge is ignored (it only applies above ₹50L income).
  • Tax rules are as in force since 23 Jul 2024 (Finance Act 2024). They can change before you redeem.
  • Returns are assumptions, not forecasts. Markets can deliver less, including losses.
